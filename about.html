Recipes Passed Down, Fire Never Lost

Our Family Story

From Hawker Carts to Our Kitchen

Three generations ago, our family ran a charcoal-fired noodle stall in a back lane of Georgetown, Penang. My grandmother cooked char kway teow for dock workers at dawn; my grandfather ran a coffee stall nearby, pouring kopi with a practiced flick. When the family later moved between Kuala Lumpur and Ipoh, each city left its mark — KL's dark, treacly Hokkien mee, Ipoh's silky bean sprouts and clear broths.

Recipe Secrets exists to keep those recipes exactly as they were: no shortcuts, no MSG-heavy substitutes, just the same wok, the same fire, and the same care our family has cooked with for over sixty years.

Meet the Chef

Chef Tan Wei Ming

Head Chef & Third-Generation Owner

Chef Tan grew up behind his grandmother's noodle stall, learning to judge a wok's heat by sound alone before he could read a recipe. After years cooking across hawker centres in Penang and Kuala Lumpur, he returned home to open Recipe Secrets — determined to cook exactly the way his grandmother did, dish by dish, with nothing rushed.

"Wok hei isn't a seasoning you can buy. It's fire, timing, and a wok that's been used long enough to remember every dish that's passed through it."
