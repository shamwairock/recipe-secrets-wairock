Our Heritage

A Taste Carried Across the Straits

Recipe Secrets began with my grandmother's charcoal stove in a Penang back lane, where the clang of the wok and the hiss of chilli paste meant dinner was close. Our family carried those recipes from Penang's hawker stalls, Ipoh's coffee shops, and KL's dark Hokkien mee carts into one kitchen — built around a simple idea: cook it the way it was always cooked, and never rush the wok.

Today we serve the same dishes with the same fire, the same hand-pounded sambal, and the same kopitiam warmth that welcomed guests decades ago.
